# Math Quest design-token contract

`assets/design/math-quest-design-tokens-v1.json` is original Math Quest project
work distributed under the repository MIT License. It is the canonical,
machine-readable target-token contract for the Mathematical Conservatory and
Workshop art direction. It contains no copied artwork, third-party palette,
font file, or package payload.

The contract is governance data, not an activated runtime stylesheet. Its
`projection.state` remains `NOT_ACTIVATED`; a future runtime projection must be
generated, hash-bound, independently reviewed, and accepted by the art-design,
feature, tutorial, accessibility, PWA, and release gates before use.
